Candidates are real — endorsements and donations are not loaded yet

Every race name, candidate name, and party label below is transcribed from the Texas Secretary of State’s Ballot Certification Report for the November 3, 2026 general election in Tarrant County. The endorsement and donation sections are empty for every candidate: that data has not been collected yet.

This page lists every race certified for the county. Your ballot will show only a subset of them — congressional, state legislative, county commissioner, and justice of the peace races are drawn by district, so which ones you vote in depends on your address.

How we source this

Every claim in this guide links to a public record. Endorsements point to the endorsing organization’s own announcement or to published news coverage. Donations point to the campaign finance report they were filed in. Nothing is included unless a link can be attached to it, and nothing here is inferred, summarized, or ranked — if a line looks wrong, open its source and judge it yourself.
